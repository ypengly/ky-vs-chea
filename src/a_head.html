    Touch

    Left stick moves. Right buttons throw jab, cross, hooks, uppercut and body shots. Hold BLOCK, tap DODGE to slip sideways, tap SPECIAL when the purple meter turns gold.

    The fight

    Three 2-minute rounds by default. Win by knockout, by three knockdowns in a round (TKO), or on the judges' cards. When you are down, mash any punch button to get up faster.

    KY hits like a truck but is slow and tires fast. CHEA is quick, never tires, and wins by volume. Dodge a punch and your next shot lands as a counter for extra damage.
